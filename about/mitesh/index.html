Volatility Research

GARCH as a dynamic risk & exit layer.

We are researching volatility-responsive systems where exits and risk logic adapt to changing market conditions rather than relying only on static distances.

Conditional volatility: model changing variance instead of assuming constant volatility.
Adaptive trailing: investigate volatility-scaled stop and trail distances.
Parameter research: compare alpha, beta and multiplier combinations across instruments and regimes.
Risk objective: reduce uncontrolled tail losses while preserving room for profitable movement.

Research Principles

Built around testing, not promises.

A

Hypothesis First

Start with a measurable market hypothesis rather than a visually attractive signal.

B

Validation

Backtests are only one layer; forward testing, robustness and costs matter.

C

Risk Engineering

Position management, exits and drawdown control are part of the model design.

D

Iteration

Models are researched, challenged, revised and discarded when evidence does not hold.

Quantitative research is experimental. Historical or simulated results do not guarantee future performance. Any partner deployment should include jurisdiction-appropriate disclosures, broker/API permissions, customer suitability controls where applicable, and regulatory/compliance review.
