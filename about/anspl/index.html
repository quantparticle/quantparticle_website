The decision in one sentence

High-trust brokerage

A repeatable growth engine

THE PROMISE WE MAKE

Disciplined transformation

  • First 90 days: prove process and establish baselines.
  • Next 9 months: scale proven channels, improve digital journeys.

02 / EXISTING CLIENTS / ACTIVATION PLAYBOOK
09 / 45

The first 10,000 conversations

Prioritise high-value and reachable cohorts—not a blast to 65,000 people.

W1–2

Select & prepare

Score clients by recency, relationship value, suitability and service gaps; finalise approved scripts.

W3–6

Contact & diagnose

Pilot 1,000 high-priority accounts with assigned RMs and structured reason codes.

W7–12

Expand & learn

Extend to remaining priority 9,000 only if contact and service economics work.

CONVERSATION

5-minute review

  • Are service and execution meeting expectations?
  • What has changed in your investing needs?
  • Would a platform walkthrough or education session help?
  • Schedule next action—never force a product.
TRACK THE REAL RESULT

Sales + satisfaction

Contact rate → meaningful conversations → appointments → authorised product discussions → 30/90-day adoption → retained contribution; log opt-outs and complaints.
10,000 is a proposed contact pool, not a confirmed reachable segment. Target high relevance and service recovery before high-volume calling.

02 / EXISTING CLIENTS / EVENT → COMMERCIAL PROCESS
11 / 45

The event is day zero—not the result

A repeatable, compliant operating sequence for every dinner, gathering or financial literacy event.

01

T−21 to T−14: audience

Select invitation-only cohort by geography, suitability and relationship owner; approve content and speaker.

02

T−7: pre-event qualification

Confirm attendance, capture learning interests with consent; assign host and attendee briefing.

03

Event day: value first

Education, market/risk context, demo desk, moderated Q&A; log explicit requests for callbacks.

04

T+1 to T+7: follow up

Thank-you, feedback, service items, optional 1:1 appointments; do not send blanket “tips”.

05

T+30 / T+90: economics

Review attendance→appointment→adoption→contribution, service satisfaction, complaints and repeated engagement.

Event growth lever: four local events / quarter in the pilot region is a test cadence, not an immediate national rollout.
